One platform, end to end

Every stage shares the same record. Information entered when the lead arrives follows the shipment all the way through to billing — no re-keying, no second system.

  • Customers & leads

    Accounts, contacts and lead sources in one place, so coordinators, sales and dispatch are all working from the same customer record.

  • Quotes & estimates

    Build priced proposals from rate tables and line items. Accepted quotes lock, and carry straight through to the move and the invoice.

  • Dispatch & operations

    The operational record of the move itself — stops, status, crew and equipment — with daily boards for the people running the day.

  • Long-haul planning

    Plan trips and shipments across drivers and lanes, with availability, zones and driver assignment built for line-haul work.

  • Driver mobile app

    Drivers see their trips and update status from the road on iOS and Android, with push notifications — so dispatch stops chasing phone calls.

  • Inventory & warehousing

    Track what is being moved room by room, and record condition at pack and at delivery — the detail that settles claims.

  • Billing & payments

    Invoices generated from accepted quotes, with payments and balances tracked against the same shipment.

  • Reporting dashboards

    Management, account and dispatch reporting, built as dashboards your team can shape around the questions you actually ask.

  • Integrations & automation

    Exchange orders and status with van lines and partners, and automate the routine steps with workflows — including a documented SDK for your own integrations.

Single sign-on, document storage and customer feedback surveys are included. An AI assistant for everyday questions is in early access.

Thirty-five years in this industry

Pegasus Software Development has been writing software for moving and storage companies since 1989. For most of that time it ran as a Windows application on the desks of coordinators, dispatchers and warehouse managers at agents across the country.

Pegasus Move Manager is that system, rebuilt as a modern cloud platform: the same operational depth — the parts that only come from decades of watching real moves go out the door — now reachable from a browser and a phone, hosted on AWS, and kept current without a desktop rollout.

Our mission is to bring advanced technology to our clients, assisting them in making each and every move more efficient and helping them exceed their customers' expectations.
